# Campus Clash — Product brief

## Positioning
User-first social gaming with optional campus competition.
**Tagline:** Play anywhere. Challenge everyone. Represent your campus.

## Audience
Initial beta for adults 18+. Students are the first acquisition cohort, but gameplay is open to anyone.

## Core loops
1. Individual: Quick Play → Score → Personal Best → Daily Challenge → Return.
2. Social: Play → Challenge Friend → Share Link → Friend Joins → Rematch.
3. Campus: Join College → Contribute Points → Campus Ranking → Rivalry → More Students Join.

## MVP
- Guest gameplay
- Bollywood Emoji Guess
- Rapid-Fire Trivia
- Score/result screen
- Local personal best
- Share challenge link
- Mobile-first responsive experience

## Next release
- Supabase Auth and profiles
- Server-created sessions and validated answers
- Friend challenge records with expiry
- Daily challenge
- Global leaderboard based only on verified scores
- Campus discovery and optional membership
- Analytics and error monitoring

## Deferred
- Live Draw & Guess, random matchmaking, public chat or voice
- Cash entry fees, cash prizes, wallets, withdrawals or paid tournaments

## Design system
- Background #0D1020; surface #171B30
- Primary #8066ED; secondary #18C6D4; accent #FFD166
- Main text #F7F7FC; muted #A4A8BC
- Rounded cards 16–24px; buttons at least 48px high
- Playful, high-energy modern gaming style; never gambling-like

## Success metrics (targets, not forecasts)
- Landing → first game: 50%+
- First-game completion: 75%+
- D1 retention: 30%+; D7: 20%+
- Challenge share rate: 25%+
- North star: Weekly Returning Players (WRP)

## Guardrails
- No signup gate before first game.
- Campus selection is optional.
- Server-validate scores before leaderboard publication.
- Initial beta is 18+.
- Publish privacy, terms, support, reporting and deletion workflows before broad promotion.
