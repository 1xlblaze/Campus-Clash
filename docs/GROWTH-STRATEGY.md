# Growing Campus Clash vs Ludo King (and similar apps)

You will not beat **Ludo King** on their turf: they have years of UA spend, creators, offline events, and a massive install base. You **can** win a **slice** — especially on campus — by being sharper on **identity, friction, and community**.

## Your unfair advantages

| Ludo King | Campus Clash angle |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Generic global theme | **Campus Clash** — hostels, fests, rival colleges, Monopoly on the same account |
| Heavy app install | **Play in browser** — share a link, no Play Store wait |
| Anonymous global match | **Room codes + WhatsApp** — play with *your* batch, society, or rival branch |
| One game | **Hub** — Ludo tonight, Monopoly tomorrow, Snakes in class break |

## Product hooks already in the app (use them in marketing)

1. **Quick 2P vs CPU** — instant session (retention for solo boredom).
2. **4P pass & play** — one phone in a hostel room (viral IRL).
3. **4P vs 3 CPU** — full table without friends online.
4. **Online room + Copy link + WhatsApp** — growth loop in group chats.
5. **Local win stats** — light progression; later tie to Supabase leaderboards per college.

## How to attract a crowd (90-day playbook)

### Week 1–2: One campus, one wedge

- Pick **one** college / city / Discord / WhatsApp group you can access.
- Message: *“Free Ludo in browser — no download. Host room, share code.”*
- Run **3 live nights** (9–11 PM): you host rooms, fix bugs live, clip funny moments.

### Week 3–4: Social proof

- Short **screen recordings** (capture + win + WhatsApp invite) → Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts.
- Caption angle: **“Hostel Ludo without installing Ludo King again.”**
- Add a **college name** in the WhatsApp share text (manual is fine at first).

### Month 2: Community events

- **Weekly hostel cup**: top wins on honor system → small prize (₹ swiggy / merch).
- **Branch vs branch** or **year vs year** using room codes (two hosts, screenshot winners).
- Partner with **one** campus club (gaming / cultural) for a sponsored “Clash Night.”

### Month 3: Retention

- **Daily streak** (already easy to add server-side): roll once per day for badge.
- **College leaderboard** (Supabase): `college_slug`, wins, optional display name.
- **Seasonal board skin** (Diwali, fest week) — differentiation Ludo King is slow to localize.

## What to build next (priority)

1. **Named college on signup** → filter leaderboard.
2. **Deep link** `/play/ludo?join=CODE` (partially supported) in every share.
3. **PWA “Add to Home Screen”** — feels like an app without store.
4. **Spectator link** (read-only room) for clips and tournaments.
5. **Hindi / Hinglish** UI toggle for North campus virality.

## Messaging that works (examples)

- *“Ludo King needs 200MB. This needs a link.”*
- *“Room code in 3 seconds — play with your batch.”*
- *“Same phone, four players — pass the phone like old school.”*
- *“Campus Clash: Ludo + Monopoly + Snakes — one site.”*

## What not to do

- Do not compete on **ads vs Gametion** until you have organic loops working.
- Do not clone **every** Ludo King mode (blitz, empire, etc.) before one campus loves the core table.
- Do not hide **Vs CPU** behind too many taps — solo players bounce fast.

## Metrics to watch

- **Share button clicks** (WhatsApp / copy link)
- **Room create → guest join** rate (online funnel)
- **Rematch** rate after a win
- **Session length** on 4P pass & play

---

**Bottom line:** Treat Ludo King as the **category leader**, not the product you copy. Be the **campus + link + friends** Ludo — then expand board games and leagues so users stay in *your* ecosystem.
